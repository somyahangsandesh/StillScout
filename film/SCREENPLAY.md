# STILLSCOUT — Short Film Screenplay
### Working title: *The Perfect Still*
**Format:** 4K · 9:16 · ~30 seconds  
**Tone:** Independent short · A24 calm · Korean drama light · Apple restraint

---

## Story

A quiet afternoon by the water.

Someone is looking — not rushing, not posting — simply *seeing*.
The world opens in layers: sky first, then the sea, then a white ship named PEACE BOAT,
then a human face that remembers why it came here.

By the end, the audience should feel:
**“I wish I could capture moments like this.”**

Then StillScout appears — not as an ad, but as the natural answer to that longing.
The practice of scouting the perfect still.

**Emotion journey:**
Silence → Wonder → Freedom → Warmth → Stillness → Recognition

## Audio Design

**Layers (built in mix):**
1. Ocean waves (bed) — constant, low
2. Wind — soft, mid presence
3. Seagulls — sparse, distant, never chatty
4. Location room tone from 0371/5168 — lightly blended for realism
5. Soft emotional piano — enters ~0:10, peaks gently under portrait, never overpowers

**Music source:** Mixkit royalty-free soft piano (`soft_piano.mp3` / Mixkit music bed)

**Mix notes:**
- Waves -18 LUFS bed
- Piano starts -28 → -22 LUFS
- Seagulls -32 LUFS peaks
- Soft 0.8s crossfades; no hard audio cuts
- Final 1s music fade under brand card

## Color Grade — “Premium Korean Drama”

- Soft highlights / gentle bloom
- Creamy whites (ship, clouds, sweater)
- Healthy natural skin (portrait)
- Muted greens in grass
- Beautiful but not neon blue sky
- Soft contrast, slightly lifted blacks
- Warm realistic sunlight — **no orange-teal**
- No oversaturation

Applied via ffmpeg curves + eq + mild unsharp bloom simulation after HLG→Rec.709 tonemap (hable).
