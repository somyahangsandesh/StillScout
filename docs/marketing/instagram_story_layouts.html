Placement rules

  1. One idea per frame — hook, then product, then CTA.
  2. Text in the middle band — never under swipe-up chrome.
  3. Brand just below the top safe zone, or in the lower live third.
  4. Max 8–12 words on screen; VO can carry the rest.
  5. Soft CTAs until store is public: Link in bio · Coming soon · Try StillScout.
