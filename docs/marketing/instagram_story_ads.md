# StillScout — Instagram Story Ads Playbook

Marketing creatives that feel like real outdoor/lifestyle ads — ready to film or design. Not in-app UI mockups.

**Brand:** StillScout  
**Tagline options:** *Scout the perfect still.* · *Find the still you’d actually post.*  
**Product truth (keep soft in ads):** Import a short clip → scout keepers → export post-ready stills. Free: **5 scouts/day**. Pro: **unlimited AI scouts** via StillScout AI.  
**Do not say:** Gemini (use **StillScout AI**).  
**Store status:** Prefer soft CTAs until App Store / public TestFlight is live.

---

## 9:16 Layout System

Design for **1080 × 1920** (or any 9:16). Stories and Story ads share the same chrome.

### Safe zones

| Zone | Approx. height | Keep clear of |
|------|----------------|---------------|
| **Top** | ~250px | Status bar, username, close (X), progress bars |
| **Bottom** | ~300px | Swipe-up / “See more” CTA, reply bar, app chrome |
| **Live area** | Middle ~60% | All primary text, product, and brand |

### Rules

1. **One idea per frame.** Hook → product → proof → CTA. Never stack three messages.
2. **Max 8–12 words on screen** per frame (headlines can be shorter).
3. **Brand placement:** Small lockup or wordmark in the **upper live area** (just below the top safe zone) or lower-third of the live area — never under the bottom CTA chrome.
4. **Text sits in the middle vertical third.** Large, high-contrast. Avoid edges and corners Instagram overlays.
5. **CTA lives in the lower live area** (above the ~300px bottom zone), or rely on Instagram’s native swipe-up / link sticker.
6. **Motion over clutter.** Prefer a single bold line + strong visual over badges, pills, and stat strips.
7. **Contrast:** Light text on dark film / vignette, or dark text on a bright still. Gold accent (`#E8C97A` / `#FFD60A`) for brand moments only — not every word.

### Flexible CTAs (pick by launch stage)

| Stage | CTA copy |
|-------|----------|
| Pre-store | `Link in bio` · `Join the waitlist` · `Coming soon` |
| Soft launch / TestFlight | `Try StillScout` · `Link in bio` |
| Public App Store | `Download StillScout` · `Get StillScout` |

## Ad Scripts

Each concept is ~**15 seconds** across **3–5 frames** (~3–5s each). Film vertical. Mix **phone screen recording** of the app with **lifestyle B-roll** (weddings, travel, kids, sports).

---

### 1. Scroll Trap  
**Hook angle:** Problem / agitation — you already shot the moment; you’re losing it in the scrub.

| Frame | ~Time | On-screen text | Visual | VO / caption (optional) |
|-------|-------|----------------|--------|-------------------------|
| 1 | 0–3s | You filmed it. | Thumb scrubbing a vertical clip — faces blur past | “You already shot the photo.” |
| 2 | 3–7s | Then you lost it in the scrub. | Frustration cut: scrubbing slows, wrong frame held | “Then you lost it in the scrub.” |
| 3 | 7–11s | StillScout finds the still. | App: scout completes → top picks grid | “StillScout finds the one you’d post.” |
| 4 | 11–15s | Scout. Polish. Post. | Final still fills frame + brand | Soft CTA |

**CTA:** `Link in bio` / `Try StillScout`  
**Tone:** Confident, slightly restless — then relief.

## Brand & Legal Notes for Creatives

- Wordmark: **StillScout** (one word, capital S twice).
- Avoid competitor comparisons and App Store ranking claims.
- Don’t promise “unlimited” cloud without context in long-form; in Stories, “unlimited AI scouts” for Pro is fine as campaign shorthand.
- No emoji spam. No purple “AI sludge” UI. Prefer dark film + gold accent to match the product.

## Filming Shot List (iPhone)

Use this as a one-afternoon checklist. Shoot everything **vertical (9:16)**. Lock exposure when possible.

### Lifestyle / outdoor

- [ ] Golden-hour couple or friends walking (wide + medium)
- [ ] One decisive laugh / hug / high-five (hold for freeze)
- [ ] Travel overlook or street dusk (slow pan)
- [ ] Kids or pets mid-motion (jump, run, turn)
- [ ] Sports peak: catch, kick, finish line, cheer
- [ ] Concert / party with clear beat hits (for Concept 5)
- [ ] Hands scrubbing Photos / camera roll (frustration beat)

### Product (screen recording)

- [ ] Import a short vertical clip
- [ ] Scout progress / scanning
- [ ] Top picks / ranked gallery
- [ ] Tap into a keeper → full still
- [ ] Optional: StillScout AI / Pro UI (no Gemini labels on screen)
- [ ] Export / share sheet moment (optional, 1–2s)

### Still plate pack (for before/after)

- [ ] “Bad scrub” still: blink, mid-blink, soft focus, awkward gesture
- [ ] “Keeper” still: peak expression, sharp eyes, good light — **same clip**
- [ ] One brand end card: dark background + StillScout wordmark + soft CTA

### Audio

- [ ] Capture clean ambient or license a short beat bed
- [ ] For Concept 5: line up freeze frame to an audible hit
- [ ] Optional VO takes: 1–2 sentences max per concept (record in a quiet room)

### Export for Stories

- [ ] 1080×1920, H.264 or HEVC
- [ ] Leave top ~250px and bottom ~300px free of critical text
- [ ] Preview in Instagram drafts before boosting as a Story ad
