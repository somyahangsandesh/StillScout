Support

How can we help?

Quick fixes for StillScout. Prefer email for account/billing; use GitHub for product bugs (no private videos in issues).

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Contact

Email: stillscout.support@gmail.com

Bugs / feedback: GitHub Issues

Common topics

  • Subscriptions / Restore: Open StillScout → paywall or Settings → Restore purchases. Or manage billing in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Free scouts: Free plan includes daily on-device scouts (UTC day). Limits reset the next UTC day.
  • AI Pro / trial: Cloud Gemini scoring needs internet. One complimentary AI trial is available when online; polish stays Pro-only.
  • Camera / library: iOS Settings → StillScout → enable Camera, Microphone, and Photos as needed.
  • Offline: Free on-device scouting still works. Cloud AI and trial require a connection.
  • Diagnostics: Settings → Copy diagnostics, then paste into your email or issue (no need to attach videos).
